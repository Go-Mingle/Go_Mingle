The problem

Restaurants absorb every no-show. Diners have nowhere social to find a table.

2,600+
licensed restaurants in Karachi
5–20%
of reservations end as no-shows
PKR 20k

For restaurants

Bookings arrive over WhatsApp and phone, with no way to enforce them. Paper books, manual no-show logs, 100 % of the loss absorbed.

For diners

No single place for curated discovery, group tables and reviews that cannot be faked.

Our solution

One focused journey, secured by a card hold on every seat.

Booking commitment

One Safepay pre-authorisation per seat: captured for a no-show, released when guests arrive.

Platform fee

Commission on every completed bill, set per restaurant category, with escrow and payouts built in.

Verified reviews

Reviews unlock only after a completed booking: a trusted corpus and the data for Phase 2.
