The O is a table. One seat is always open.

Tables on GoMingle seat two people or ten, so the mark never counts heads. It shows a round table, the people already around it, and one open seat drawn as an outline: the place that is waiting for you.

Ember ring: the table, drawn in one stroke on load.
Filled seats: guests already booked, candle and mint alternating.
Open seat: the outline that pulses once. It is the call to action, in the logo.
